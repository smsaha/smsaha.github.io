COPY-AND-PASTE SNIPPETS FOR FUTURE UPDATES
==========================================

1) NEW PUBLICATION (publications.html)
Find the year block (for example 2026). If it does not exist, copy a whole year block
(from <ul class="dated"> to its closing </ul>) and change the year. Then add inside the inner list:

<li style="margin-bottom:.9rem"><p class="pub">Author A, <strong>Saha, S. M.</strong>, &amp; Author C. Title of the paper. <em>Journal Name</em>, 12(3), 100&ndash;120. <a href="https://doi.org/PASTE-DOI">DOI</a></p></li>

2) LINK TO A PAPER (any page)
Add at the end of the entry:  <a href="PASTE-URL">PDF</a>

3) ABSTRACT UNDER A WORKING PAPER (research.html)
Paste just before the closing </div> of that paper's entry:

<details><summary>Abstract</summary><p>Paste abstract text here.</p></details>

4) NEWS ITEM (index.html, under News)
Paste as the first item in the News list:

<li><span class="when">2026</span><div class="what"><p>Your news sentence.</p></div></li>

5) NEW AWARD OR PRESENTATION (cv.html)
Copy an existing <li> line in the same section and change the year and text.

6) TEACHING COURSE (teaching.html)
Delete the <!-- and --> around the course template and edit the text.

7) AFTER EVERY CHANGE
Change "Last updated" in the footer of each page you edited (search for "Last updated").
