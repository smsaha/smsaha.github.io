Sourav Mohan Saha

PhD student in Agricultural and Applied Economics, University of Missouri

I study production economics and efficiency in agriculture and aquaculture. My dissertation research examines how disease, mitigation, and management shape the productivity of coastal shrimp farms in Bangladesh.

I am advised by Theodoros Skevas in the Division of Applied Social Sciences. Before my PhD, I was a Lecturer at Khulna Agricultural University, Bangladesh.

Download CV (PDF) smsaha@missouri.edu

Research interests

  • Production economics and efficiency analysis
  • Damage control and disease economics in aquaculture
  • Environmental efficiency of farming systems
  • International trade in aquaculture products
  • Food safety and consumer demand
  • Climate adaptation and rural livelihoods in Bangladesh
