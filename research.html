Event-specific efficiency of coastal shrimp farms in Bangladesh

Measures how disease and mortality events affect the technical efficiency of shrimp (gher) farms, using survey data from the IFPRI Shrimp Farms 2023 survey.

Studies how the early mortality disease shock (AHPND/EMS) reshaped shrimp export patterns, using a country-year panel of major shrimp-exporting countries.
